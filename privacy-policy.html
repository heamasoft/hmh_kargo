Privacy Policy

HMH Kargo — operated by Heama Soft

Last updated: August 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Heama Soft (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the HMH Kargo mobile application (the “App”), a shop‑and‑ship service that lets customers in Iraq buy products from international online stores and have them delivered locally.

By creating an account or using the App, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Who we are

HMH Kargo is developed and operated by Heama Soft, based in Zakho, Duhok, Iraq. For any privacy question or request, contact us at heama.soft@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

We only collect the information needed to create your account, process your orders, and deliver your parcels.

CategoryExamplesWhy we collect it
Account information Phone number, name, city, and (optionally) email address To create and secure your account and to identify you as a customer
Verification (OTP) Your phone number and a one‑time verification code sent via WhatsApp To confirm you own the phone number during sign‑up and password reset
Delivery address Recipient name, governorate, city, street, and contact phone To deliver your orders to the correct location
Orders & shopping activity Products you add, order details, item prices, and order history To place, price, track, and manage your orders
Wallet & transactions Your in‑app wallet balance and a ledger of credits/debits (top‑ups, order payments, refunds) To operate your prepaid balance and show your transaction history
Device & notifications A push‑notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) To send you order updates and alerts
We do NOT collect payment‑card details. The App does not process credit or debit cards. Top‑ups are arranged through cash or approved local methods (e.g. FIB), and orders are paid from your in‑app wallet or cash on delivery. Your in‑app “wallet balance” is an account credit managed by Heama Soft, not a bank account.

3. How we use your information

  • Create, secure, and manage your account.
  • Verify your identity using one‑time codes (OTP) via WhatsApp.
  • Capture, price, place, and track the products you order.
  • Calculate shipping and manage your in‑app wallet and refunds.
  • Deliver your parcels to your chosen address.
  • Send order status updates and important notifications.
  • Provide customer support and prevent fraud or abuse.

4. When shopping from external stores

The App includes an in‑app browser so you can view international store websites (such as Shein, Trendyol, Zara, Mango, H&M and others) and select the product you want. When you choose a product, the App captures only the details needed to fulfil your order — such as the product link, title, image, price, and the options you pick (size, colour). Your browsing inside those third‑party sites is governed by those sites’ own privacy policies; we do not control them.

5. How we share your information

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only with the service providers required to operate the App:

ProviderWhat is sharedPurpose
Meta (WhatsApp Business / Cloud API) Your phone number and the OTP code To deliver your verification code by WhatsApp
Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) Your device push token To deliver push notifications
Our hosting provider (secure server) Your account, orders, and wallet data To store and process your data for the service

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users and our service.

6. Data retention

We keep your account and order information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service and meet legal, accounting, or reporting requirements. When you ask us to delete your account, we remove or anonymise your personal data except where we are required to keep certain records (for example, completed order/transaction records).

7. Security

Your data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored on secured servers. Access is restricted to authorised personnel. One‑time codes are stored only in a hashed form and expire quickly. While no system can be guaranteed 100% secure, we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Access & correction: view or update your name, city, email, and delivery addresses in the App at any time.
  • Deletion: request deletion of your account and personal data by emailing heama.soft@gmail.com or contacting our support numbers below.
  • Notifications: turn off push notifications from your device settings.

9. Children’s privacy

HMH Kargo is intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will reflect the latest revision. Significant changes will be communicated through the App or our website.
